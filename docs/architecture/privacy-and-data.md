# Privacy and data handling design

Proposed behavior only: storage and deletion mechanisms are not implemented.

Manual Style DNA and closet entry must work without a portrait. Photo transfer requires affirmative purpose-specific opt-in and clear retention/deletion information. Validate type, size and decodability before estimation; source limits are planning values, not enforced settings.

Keep raw portraits in private temporary storage behind authorized paths. Do not transmit them to retailers, logs or public exports. Return uncertain styling suggestions and permit correction. Do not infer identity, health, ethnicity or body measurements. Item photographs are separate from portraits but may also contain personal data.

Before collecting real photographs, agree on retention, hosting, guest ownership, derived-artifact deletion and backups. Verify active-storage deletion and document backup behavior. A plan is not an implemented privacy policy.

Catalog records need source attribution, usage permissions, image rights and as-of metadata. Secrets stay outside the project; `.env.example` has blank values. This is an engineering plan, not a jurisdiction-specific legal assessment.
